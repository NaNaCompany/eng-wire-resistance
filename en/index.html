FORMULA

R = ρ_e L / A

Assumptions

Use ρ_e,L,A>0. Assume a DC conductor of uniform material and area. Contact resistance and AC skin effects are excluded. Areas entered in mm² are converted to m².

Displayed values are floating-point approximations. Measurement uncertainty and design safety factors are not calculated.
